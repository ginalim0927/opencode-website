Research for Defense Against AI Agent Attacks

As AI agents grow more capable, ensuring they act safely and securely becomes critical. This page summarizes our research agenda and early progress on defending against rogue or harmful AI agents.

Proposed Research Plan

Our proposed plan lays out the path from threat modeling to deployed defense mechanisms for autonomous AI agents.

  • Map the threat surface of agentic systems.
  • Develop detection and containment strategies.
  • Validate approaches through red-teaming and simulation.

Progress

A summary of milestones reached so far in the project.

  • Drafted initial threat taxonomy for agent attacks.
  • Established baseline agent behavior benchmarks.
  • Completed early red-team evaluation rounds.
