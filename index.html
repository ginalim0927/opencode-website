Research for Defense Against AI Agent Attacks

As AI agents grow more capable, ensuring they act safely and securely becomes critical. This page summarizes our research agenda and early progress on defending against rogue or harmful AI agents.

Proposed Research Plan

We propose defending against autonomous AI agents through dynamically generated decoy files, and measuring how such defenses affect attack success and runtime.

  • Generate decoy files dynamically for targeted attack scenarios.
  • Test autonomous agent behavior against decoy deployments.
  • Measure attack success rates and runtime.

Progress

Initial experiments into how autonomous agents behave when confronted with decoys, with successes and failures tracked during testing.

  • Run initial experiments with agent behavior and decoy files.
  • Track successes and failures across test runs.
  • Investigate how increasing the number of decoy files affects performance.
